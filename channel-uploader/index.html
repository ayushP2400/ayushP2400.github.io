Channel Uploader

Channel Uploader is a private command-line tool that Ayush Pathak uses on his own computer to publish his own finished videos to his own three YouTube channels: Steppe Lives, Leela Dham and Zoom Depth. It is not offered to, sold to or used by anyone else.

What it does

  • Uploads a finished video file with a resumable upload (videos.insert).
  • Sets the title, description, tags, category, language, "made for kids", the altered-content declaration and a scheduled publish time.
  • Sets the custom thumbnail (thumbnails.set) and adds the video to the channel's own playlists (playlistItems.insert).
  • Adds the Hindi subtitle track (captions.insert) and the Hindi title/description localization (videos.update), and reads back the uploaded video's status (videos.list).
  • Reads the signed-in channel's ID (channels.list, mine=true) to confirm it is uploading to the right channel.

How sign-in works

The owner signs in once per channel with Google (OAuth 2.0, scopes youtube.upload and youtube). The access token is stored only on the owner's computer with owner-only file permissions. Access can be removed at any time at Google Account → Third-party connections.
